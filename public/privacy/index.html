2. Information We Collect

To enable workforce management, biometric attendance logging, and enterprise telecalling CRM operations, we collect the following data:

  • Personal Identity Data: Employee full name, unique employee ID (empCode), official email address, mobile phone number, job role (Telecaller, Team Leader, HR, Admin), department/squad assignment, emergency contact, date of birth, and blood group.
  • Camera & Biometric Verification Selfies: Live facial photographs taken strictly during attendance clock-in and clock-out to verify employee physical presence and prevent proxy attendance. We never access the camera in the background.
  • Precise Location Coordinates: Device GPS coordinates (latitude, longitude) captured strictly at the instant of attendance check-in and check-out to verify the employee is physically present within the designated office geofence. We do NOT track employee movements continuously in the background.
  • Telecalling CRM Communications: When telecallers place outgoing sales calls to company-assigned leads, the platform records dial timestamps, call durations, call dispositions, and deal payment submission records.
  • Device Identifiers: Operating system version, device model, and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens for delivery of push alerts.

4. Purpose of Data Processing

Your personal data is processed strictly for legitimate corporate employment purposes:

  • Verifying shift attendance, working hours, overtime, and leave balances.
  • Calculating monthly compensation, salary advances, and generating official payslips.
  • Distributing sales leads and monitoring enterprise conversion milestones.
  • Validating deal closure payment vouchers and client remittances.
  • Securing corporate infrastructure against fraudulent access and proxy attendance.

5. Data Security Standards

All data transmitted between the mobile application and our servers is secured using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS encryption). Databases are protected by role-based access control (RBAC), restricting employee compliance logs strictly to authorized Super Administrators and certified HR personnel.
