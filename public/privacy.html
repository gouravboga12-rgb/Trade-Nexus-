1. About Us

This Privacy Policy explains how Trade Nexus Trade Smart ("we", "our", or "us") handles personal data when you use our mobile application and employee management platform.

Our application is an internal workplace portal designed for authorized team members to check daily attendance, view company documents, and manage regular work tasks.

2. What Information We Collect

We collect only the basic information necessary for everyday work functions:

  • Profile & Account Details: Name, employee ID, company email, phone number, and assigned team role.
  • Attendance Verification: When you punch in or punch out for your shift, the app records your check-in time, optional attendance selfie, and your location coordinates to confirm presence at the office. We do not track your location continuously in the background.
  • Work Records: Shift records, leave applications, payslip history, and assigned task statuses.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for internal workplace operations:

  • To record and verify daily attendance and work hours.
  • To display your employee profile, digital ID card, and company offer letters.
  • To send you important work updates, attendance notifications, and shift alerts.
  • To ensure workplace safety and prevent unauthorized access to corporate accounts.

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data with any third parties or advertisers.

4. Data Storage & Security

All information is securely transmitted using encrypted HTTPS (TLS) connections. Access to employee data is strictly limited to authorized administrative and HR personnel through secure credentials.

5. Account & Data Deletion Requests

You have full control over your personal data. If you leave the organization or want your account and personal records permanently removed, you can submit a deletion request at any time.

How to request deletion:

  1. Send an email to: hr@tradenexustradesmart.in
  2. Include your full name and employee ID.
  3. Mention that you want your account and personal records deleted.

Upon receiving your request, our HR desk will verify your details and permanently delete your profile, attendance photos, and account credentials from our active database.
